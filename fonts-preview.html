The three R's of good lighting

You don't need more light to feel safe — you need better light. It comes down to three simple ideas: reduce, restrict, and redirect.

Visual stress

Intense glare and blue-white light strain the eyes and make it harder to see clearly at night.

Light in your home

Unshielded fixtures spill into bedrooms and yards, disrupting sleep for people who never asked for it.
